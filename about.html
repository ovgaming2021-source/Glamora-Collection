Longevity & Care

How to Care for Your Cotton Dresses

Pure natural cotton matures gracefully with simple gentle handling. Follow these easy steps to keep your colors vibrant for years:

Cold Water Wash

Machine wash on gentle cycle or hand wash at 30°C to preserve fiber elasticity.

Mild Liquid Detergent

Avoid chlorine bleaches or harsh optical brighteners that can strip natural pigments.

Shade Dry

Dry inside out under indirect shade. Natural cotton dries quickly in a breezy spot.

Warm Steam Iron

Iron on reverse side with light steam for that crisp, boutique-fresh look.
